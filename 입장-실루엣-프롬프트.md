# 경기장 진입로 실루엣 — 이미지 생성 프롬프트

한국 축구 베스트11 진입 컷용.  
비율은 도구에서 **1:1**. (프롬프트 본문에는 비율을 적지 않는다.)

---

## 영문 프롬프트 (생성기 투입)

```
A cinematic match-day photograph from deep inside a long modern stadium player tunnel, looking toward the pitch exit.

CAMERA & SPACE
- Camera set far back at the rear of a long corridor. Strong vanishing-point perspective; walls and floor converge toward a distant exit.
- The tunnel feels dozens of meters long. The lower and middle of the frame are empty dark floor and clean dark walls — a long empty approach before any figures appear.
- A squad of players walks in the middle distance: large enough to read as full human silhouettes with heads, shoulders, and legs, still with a long stretch of tunnel left before they reach the light.

PLAYERS
- Men's Korean national football team walking away from the camera into the stadium for kickoff, backs to the camera.
- Photographic silhouettes with real three-dimensional body volume — East Asian athletic male builds, short dark hair, natural strides.
- Every player in the same deep red Korea home match kit: red jersey, red shorts, red socks, boots. Soft deep-red rim light traces shoulders, heads, and kit edges so the whole squad glows consistently red.
- Match-ready kit only: short sleeves, bare arms, empty hands, natural walking arm swing. They are going out to play.

TUNNEL
- Almost black interior. Clean modern architecture — smooth panels, neat ceiling lines, reflective dark floor. Quiet, premium, well-kept.

EXIT / STADIUM
- At the far end, the exit opens onto a living night stadium. Soft volumetric light and haze pour inward from outside.
- Through the mouth, the stadium color spills in: vivid green pitch, yellow and red seating tiers, floodlights, soft crowd color — a real match atmosphere beyond the silhouettes, not a blank white glare.

STYLE
- Deep chiaroscuro: dark corridor, bright exit, red-rimmed silhouettes.
- Sports cinema still, wide low angle, slight film grain, photoreal photograph quality.
```

## 재생성 시 우선 보강 문장

통로가 짧아질 때:

```
Long empty dark floor fills the foreground; the squad appears only in the far half of the tunnel.
```

선수 크기가 점처럼 될 때:

```
Middle-distance silhouettes, full body readable, still short of the exit.
```

킷 색이 흔들릴 때:

```
Identical deep red home kits on every player; red rim light only.
```

입구가 흰 빛만 될 때:

```
Green pitch and yellow-red stands visible through the exit with soft stadium color.
```
